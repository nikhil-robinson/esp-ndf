Why: with 251-byte PDUs the IDF central ends every connection event with our server after 5-6 exchanges (~12.8 ms: 6 central PDUs received, 5 of ours acknowledged), whatever the
connection interval (20/50/100 ms), the requested connection event length (0.625 ms units 16..768 in ble_connect/ble_update) or the PHY; the same central takes 18 PDUs per event (43 ms) from the
IDF server. Not found on our side: CS words (+0x1E, +0x20, +0x28, +0x2E), exchange-table words 5-7, core registers 0xE4/0xEC/0xF8/0x2CC/0x330/0x374 set to IDF's values, DLE buffers: all without effect.
Open: needs an air capture (third board) of both servers' events. Small PDUs are not affected (events of 25 ms with 45 PDUs).
2026-11-03, also without effect: 16 long TX slots instead of 8 (the event carries ~6.7 PDUs either way, so the chain is not the limit) and the CS format word 0x0603 of the IDF server's link (bits 9-10 set once the data length is extended). The air capture is possible now: the esp-ndf central (ble_central) can be the master for our own server, but the open question is the IDF central's decision, so a third board listening on the data channels is still the way.
