2026-11-03 SOLVED. The 244-byte gap was two of our own limits, not the IDF central:
(1) the TX chain was only topped up at the event end (8 long slots = 7-8 PDUs per event): the RX interrupt (core status bit 2) now refills the chain inside the event (ble_hw.c service_, tx_load with `live`);
(2) CS+0x20 is the longest the event may run in 625 us slots, and ble_hw.c wrote the RX ring size (48) into it: the core ends the event when the exchanges add up to 48 slots = 30 ms (13 PDUs of 251 bytes,
    30 of 67 bytes, 21 at 2M; 24 -> exactly half). IDF's slave CS has 0x49 (73) at a 50 ms interval. maxev_slots() now gives interval - 7 slots (half the interval while advertising / initiating, shortened
    to the next other link's event). The earlier "no effect" experiments (CS words, 16 long slots, 0x0603) were all hidden behind the other limit.
How it was found: per-event trace of the TX chain and the RX ring (13 exchanges, last response unacknowledged, central's last RX descriptor = sync timeout), JTAG dumps of both boards' EM
(bench/idf_bleprobe/ocd_em_central.py, data/em_central_* / em_server_*: CS word +0x20 0x30 vs 0x49), exchanges x duration = 48 slots for 60 / 150 / 244-byte PDUs and 2M.
